RetinaPrep: retinal fundus leakage audit

A curation and leakage-audit pipeline for retinal fundus datasets, built around one finding: leakage in retinal imaging has three rungs, not one, each necessary and, alone, insufficient. Image-level splitting leaks patients (42.0% of ODIR-5K's patients land on both sides of a naive split). Patient-level splitting leaks sites instead — a classifier recovers which of 20 camera/centre clusters an image came from with 84% accuracy from a patient-grouped split alone, and site correlates with diagnosis (chi2=115.18, p=8.8e-16). Only site-level splitting isolates the signal: holding out entire sites drops AUROC by 0.0557 — over 3x the patient-level effect, Bonferroni-significant, and shown directly (not just diagnosed) to generalize across most held-out sites, not just the one that happened to land in the original split. Full write-up, headline numbers, and methodology are in the GitHub repository's README and WALKTHROUGH.md.
