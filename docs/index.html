RetinaPrep: retinal fundus leakage audit

Fundus datasets leak at three levels, and each level of protection is insufficient for the next — measured end to end on two independent datasets: ODIR-5K (6,392 images, multi-centre Chinese) and EyePACS (35,126 images, a US telemedicine screening network).

The third row is the one to read twice: patient-grouped splitting — what most papers mean by "we split properly" — cannot catch one photograph filed under two patient IDs. In EyePACS, 58% of duplicate clusters straddle a patient-grouped split anyway.

Stated up front rather than buried: the downstream AUROC cost of level-1 leakage is small and did not replicate cleanly; "site" is a resolution-derived proxy, not camera metadata, in both datasets; every individual held-out site's confidence interval spans zero; and the duplicate finding shows contamination exists, not that any specific published result was affected. Full write-up, headline numbers and methodology are in the GitHub repository's README and WALKTHROUGH.md.
